Model formulas, inclusions and exclusions

Annual service revenue = customers × monthly service fee × 12. Gross profit = service revenue × service gross margin. Annual operating result = gross profit − monthly fixed opex × 12. Break-even customers = round up(monthly opex ÷ monthly fee ÷ margin).

Gross funding = (monthly opex × runway months + setup) × (1 + contingency). Steady-state funding = (max(0, monthly opex − monthly customer gross profit) × months + setup) × (1 + contingency).

Variable service delivery costs sit inside gross margin. Fixed opex must exclude those same costs. Cloud and EDA charges are customer-paid or separately disclosed pass-through. Onboarding revenue, cash on hand, tax, debt, capitalized development, working capital and Phase 2/3 are excluded. Pricing, staffing costs and margin require validation.
